# What's New in v0.1.0 🎉

Welcome to our first major update! We’ve been working hard behind the scenes to turn this into an app you’ll love using every day. From a brand-new shopping experience to a much cleaner look, there’s a lot to explore.


## ✨ New Things You Can Do

### Complete Your Shopping Journey
You can now go from browsing to buying in just a few taps. We’ve added a brand-new shopping cart, a smooth checkout process, and a handy order history page so you can keep track of all your favorite finds.
**What it means for you:** It’s like moving from a catalog to a full-service boutique where you can actually take your favorites home.

### Navigate with Ease
We’ve added "breadcrumbs" at the top of your screens. This little trail helps you see exactly where you are in the app and makes it super easy to jump back to a previous page without getting lost.
**What it means for you:** It’s like having a "You Are Here" map that follows you around so you never lose your way.

### More Ways to Interact
We’ve introduced a whole set of new tools like sliders for adjusting settings, switches for toggling options, and helpful little pop-up tips that explain how things work.
**What it means for you:** The app now feels more like a high-end tool that responds perfectly to your touch.


## 🐛 We Fixed Some Annoyances

### Smarter Error Screens
You may have noticed those frustrating moments when something didn't load quite right. We’ve replaced those confusing errors with much friendlier messages that actually help you get back on track.

### Better for Everyone
We’ve put a lot of love into making the app easier to use for everyone. This includes better support for those who use screen assistants and making sure all our buttons and text are easy to see and tap.


## 💅 Feels Smoother

### A Fresh Coat of Paint
We’ve updated our entire color palette and typography. Everything looks sharper, more consistent, and is much easier on the eyes, whether you’re browsing in the morning or late at night.

### Snappier Loading
Instead of staring at a blank screen while things load, you’ll now see "shimmer" placeholders that show you exactly where content is about to appear. It makes the whole app feel much faster and more responsive.

### Polished Layouts
We’ve tidied up the spacing and alignment across every screen. The app feels less cluttered and more organized, letting the things you care about stand out.
